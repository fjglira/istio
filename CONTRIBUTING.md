# Contribution guidelines

So you want to hack on Istio? Yay! Please refer to Istio's overall
[contribution guidelines](https://github.com/istio/community/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)
to find out how you can help.

## Use of generative AI

Contributions made with the help of AI tools are welcome, but contributors remain fully responsible
for them. Please read our [AI Policy](AI-POLICY.md) before submitting AI-assisted changes.
